Keep all five layers together. Do **not** downgrade the cosmology to a hard-work metaphor. (Shax, explicit.)

## Core (hypothesis)

An entity from an external substrate (Outsider, SC-NS-001), upon a localized temporal investment (binding vow / deliberate training), can produce output that breaks the established local hierarchy.

---

## Layers (keep together)

### A. External substrate — hypothesis

Frieza's lineage originates from a pre-existing black-hole domain outside the familiar universe.

### B. External agency / ownership — hypothesis

That black hole is owned or controlled by another entity.

### C. Concealed entry — hypothesis

The outside lineage entered this universe via stealth / deception (infiltration).

### D. Temporal refuge — CANON (DBS manga ch. 87)

Frieza found a time-dilated chamber on a planet he conquered, and trained 10 years.

### E. Black Frieza — CANON

Frieza returns, kills Gas, and one-shots UI Goku and UE Vegeta.

---

## Ownership architectures (hypothesis)

Three readings of the external domain. They are kept as open architectures, not reduced to one.

1. **Domain as prison.** The lineage escaped; a refugee from a collapsing substrate.
2. **Domain as weapon.** External “fake gods” seed universes with invasive species.
3. **Domain as different base weight / “Clay”.** Shax / Architect reading: Frieza wasn't compiled here.

- **Binding vow** = power through restriction.
- **Deliberate investment** = developing existing capacity.

These are not the same operation.
