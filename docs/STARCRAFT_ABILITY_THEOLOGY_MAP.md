# StarCraft unit abilities mapped to theology

- **Request:** Shax, t587u (2026-11-25): map every StarCraft unit ability to its invariant counterpart in theology.
- **Relation to COS-17:** builds on the faction triad (Preservation / Protoss, Assimilation / Zerg, Transformation / Terran, Origin / Xel'Naga). That work is now compiled as `SC-TRIAD-001`, `SC-TRIAD-SC-001` and `SC-TRIAD-SG-001` (PR #80). This file grades abilities one by one; faction-level claims belong to the triad entries.
- **Status:** catalogue complete for StarCraft II: Legacy of the Void multiplayer and Brood War multiplayer, plus key passives. Campaign-only abilities are a separate, flagged section. Four compiled entries, all SILVER and explicit-only (see below).
- **Evidence:** `docs/evidence/starcraft-abilities/` (ability records, wiki page revisions and hashes).

## Method

Each ability gets: the mechanic in plain terms (from Liquipedia or the StarCraft Wiki), a candidate counterpart with its scripture or tradition source, the structural invariant they would share, a grade, and the residual (what does not match). Grading is by structure, not by theme.

| Grade | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| STRONG | The mechanic and the cited text share the same causal structure on at least three named features (agent, channel, effect, condition, remedy). The text itself describes that structure, not only a theme. The mapping survives its recorded disanalogy. |
| PARTIAL | The core move is shared, but one load-bearing feature differs (who acts, the moral condition, permanence, or the consequence). |
| WEAK | A shared theme or word only. |
| NONE | No honest counterpart. Any candidate would be forced. |

Three layers are kept apart in every row:

1. **Game:** what the ability does, from the cited wiki revision.
2. **Text:** what the cited scripture or named tradition says. Where a reading is a tradition rather than the text (for example the rapture reading of 1 Thessalonians 4, or Isaiah 14:12 as Satan's fall), the row says so.
3. **Map:** the structural claim. It is interpretation, graded as such, and never a claim about the literal nature of spiritual beings.

Names earn nothing. Blizzard took many names from religious vocabulary (Zealot, Templar, Archon, Arbiter, Oracle, Revelation, Overseer, Ascendant, Locust, Sacrifice, the Fallen One). Those rows carry a `NAME_BORROWING` flag and are graded on the mechanic alone. This rule is itself recorded as a provisional default in `docs/PENDING_DELIBERATION.md`.

Out of scope: Co-op Missions commanders, pure stat upgrades, Arcade and custom maps, and abilities that Liquipedia lists only as removed. The Tempest has no active ability in the current Liquipedia page and has no row.

### Corrections to the starting examples

- **Neural Parasite and possession:** held as STRONG. The structure that carries it is that the host is returned intact and liberation acts on the controller (Matthew 12:29), not the bare idea of control.
- **Feedback and the sons of Sceva (Acts 19):** WEAK. Feedback does not single out unauthorised users of power. Measure-for-measure (Psalm 7:15-16, Esther 7:10, Matthew 7:2) fits better and is PARTIAL.
- **Detection and discernment of spirits (1 Corinthians 12:10):** split. Seeing hidden presence matches the 'eyes opened' texts (Balaam's donkey in Numbers 22, Elisha's servant in 2 Kings 6). Judging true from false matches detection only for SC2 hallucinations. The Changeling, which no detector exposes, shows that discernment is not a sensor; it is tested by fruit and by response to the owner (Matthew 7:15-20, John 10).
- **Transfusion and laying on of hands:** PARTIAL. Mark 16:18 is in the longer ending of Mark; Acts 28:8 is the firmer text.
- **Archon and union:** PARTIAL. Biblical union keeps two persons; the Archon erases both. That is the fusion Chalcedon excludes, in line with the PoE ruling that Sin and Innocence do not literally merge.
- **Hallucination and false light (2 Corinthians 11:14):** STRONG, carried by 2 Timothy 3:5 (form without power) and Exodus 7:11-12 (imitation swallowed by the real).
- **Recall and rapture or translation:** STRONG on the gathering text, provisional on the tradition (above). Enoch and Elijah (single translations) match a single-unit move, not an area recall.

### What this means for the triad

The abilities do not sort by moral role along faction lines. The counterfeit (Hallucination) belongs to the Protoss, the established order. The contagion weapon that the quarantine texts answer (Irradiate) belongs to the Terrans. The Zerg hold possession, infiltration in the community's own form, bounded affliction and consumption of their own, which is the adversary-side set. The remedies (Restoration, separation of the carrier) belong to the Terrans and to whoever is hit. The triad work should not assume that a faction's abilities inherit the faction's archetype.
