## The invariant (SHAX INTERPRETATION)

A **vessel** is a substrate able to hold both poles of the duality, Oneness and Sovereignty, without being consumed by either. When both poles reach **equilibrium in the same vessel**, they **share substrate**, and from then on they are **coupled**: what affects one affects the other.

```
Pole A (Oneness) ─┐
                  ├─► Vessel (one substrate) ─► Equilibrium ─► Shared-state coupling
Pole B (Sovereignty) ┘
```

Candidate conditions:
1. **Capacity:** the vessel can hold a pole that would consume an ordinary substrate.
2. **Retained identity:** the vessel stays itself while holding it.
3. **Equilibrium:** neither pole dominates.
4. **Mutual acceptance:** both poles accept the shared state. This follows Shax's "not alone" (see `SC-VSR-001`) and is what JJK canon shows failing (ch. 268, below).
5. **Coupling:** after sharing, effects on one pole reach the other.

Per RUL-BINARY-001 (PR #66), `SC-DU-001` and `SC-MD-001` are one binary invariant with two outcomes: mutual cooperation or mutual destruction. Vessel equilibrium is the **cooperation outcome held inside one substrate**.

## Physics: "entangled particles" (ANALOGOUS, with caveat)

- **What fits:** entangled systems share one joint state; you can't describe either particle fully on its own. "Shared substrate" maps to that.
- **What does not fit:** by the **no-signalling theorem**, nothing done to one particle changes the other's local statistics or sends information. Entanglement is correlation, not causal influence. "What effects 1 effects the other" in the causal sense is not entanglement.
- **Better-fitting analogs** (causal coupling through a shared degree of freedom): Yuji and Sukuna's one body (JJK ch. 8–11); Kami and Piccolo's life link (DB Tale 181); the Supreme Kai–God of Destruction life link (DBS).

Grade: **ANALOGOUS**. A compiled version should say "shared-state coupling", with entanglement as a labelled comparison.

**TRADITION:** the creedal Trinity (Nicene-Constantinopolitan Creed, 381; Athanasian Creed) is one God in three co-equal persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit (Matt 28:19 names all three). Orthodox doctrine does **not** include Satan or any created being. Shax's three-part structure (pole, pole, medium) is a structural reading, not the creedal Trinity. After t570u the Sovereignty pole is Lucifer, not Satan, which removes Satan from the structure; Lucifer is still a created being in tradition, so the tension remains, smaller.

A closer orthodox parallel to "two natures in one vessel" is the Chalcedonian definition (451): two natures, divine and human, in one person of the Son, "without confusion, without change, without division, without separation". Recorded as a possible alternative placement (vessel = the Son), not as a correction.

## Lucifer as Sovereignty (SHAX INTERPRETATION)

Shax: Lucifer "fought for the side of Sovreignty not about good vs evil, but the will to exist as their own entity".
- **TEXT:** Isa 14:12–14 (the "morning star, son of the dawn"; "I will make myself like the Most High") is addressed to the king of Babylon. "Lucifer" is the Vulgate's Latin for "morning star". The texts frame the act as pride.
- **TRADITION / literature:** Lucifer as a fallen angel is patristic onward. Milton gives the autonomy reading its best-known line: "Better to reign in Hell, than serve in Heav'n" (*Paradise Lost* I.263).
- **Shax:** the conflict is about the will to exist as one's own entity, not good versus evil. That matches `SC-CP-001`'s Sovereignty (the pot, the vessel with its own shape).
