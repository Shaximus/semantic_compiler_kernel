> **Unity must not require the destruction of identity.**

- A legitimate creator does not need captivity to preserve creation.
- A legitimate authority does not need immunity from criticism.
- A legitimate relationship does not need coercion to preserve connection.
- A legitimate correction mechanism does not need to destroy what it corrects.
- A legitimate system can recognize its own mistakes without collapsing.

Unity without absorption · authority without ownership · alignment without forced obedience · love without captivity · criticism without hostility · restoration without erasure · creation without compulsory submission.

### 30.5 Hard constraints and graded dimensions

**Hard constraints** — each is necessary; failing any one fails the arrangement regardless of the rest:

1. **Identity preservation** — entities are not overwritten or erased without consent.

**Graded dimensions** — scored 0 (fails), 1 (partial), 2 (meets): agency, consent, transparency, consequences, restoration, sustainability.

| Result | Condition |
|---|---|

| **CONDITIONAL** | All hard constraints met; one or more graded dimensions at 0 — named and correctable |
| **ILLEGITIMATE** | Any hard constraint failed |

A system that scores 2 on every graded dimension but traps its participants is ILLEGITIMATE: the hard constraints are not tradeable.

## PART 33: THE EVIDENCE AND FALSIFICATION ENGINE

### 33.1 TIG Check 4 — Predictive Discrimination

Alongside Consistency, Isomorphism and Corroboration: **can this interpretation predict something that competing interpretations do not?** Outputs:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DISCRIMINATING** | A pre-specified prediction distinguishes the interpretation from alternatives, and it is observed |
| **NON-DISCRIMINATING** | Competing interpretations explain the same observation equally |
| **FAILED** | A pre-specified prediction is not observed |
| **PENDING** | The prediction is recorded; the test has not produced an outcome yet |

Predictions are recorded, with a timestamp, **before** the observation wherever possible. A prediction written after the outcome is known cannot be scored DISCRIMINATING.

### 33.2 Three confidence values

| Confidence | Measures |
|---|---|
| Structural | Is the mapping internally coherent? |
| Empirical | Are the supporting observations accurate? |
| Interpretive | Is the proposed explanation the right one? |

Reported separately, never collapsed into one number. A GOLD rating certifies the first.

Observation → Corroboration → Structural correspondence → Predictive validation → Independent replication → Explanatory superiority.
