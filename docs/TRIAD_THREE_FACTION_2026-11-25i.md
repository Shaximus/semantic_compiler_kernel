## Where the triad holds

1. **Consent separates symbiosis from possession.** This is the strongest result, and it holds across Goa'uld/Tok'ra; Dragon Ball absorption (Buu, Cell: `SC-LU-CELL-001`) versus fusion (`SC-LU-VEGETA-001`, the cooperation branch); and, as tradition, possession (Mark 5:1-20) versus indwelling by invitation (Rev 3:20).
2. **The possessor sits behind the possessed host.** Examples: Amon behind the Overmind; the Goa'uld behind the Jaffa; Babidi behind Majin Vegeta; Diablo inside the Dark Wanderer; Baal sealed in Tal Rasha (`SC-VSR-001` notes).
3. **The third faction slides into assimilation when it uses the enemy as an instrument.** In StarCraft: the Sons of Korhal's psi emitters, and the UED sent to enslave the Overmind. In Path of Exile: Malachai and the Vaal capturing the Beast (`SC-POE-MAL-001`, `SC-POE-VAL-001`).
4. **Two poles from one source (DU/MD, one invariant).** Examples: form and essence from the xel'naga; Ancients and Ori from the Alterans; Sin and Innocence (`SC-POE-DU-001`, `SC-POE-MRG-001`); Anu and Tathamet.
5. **RUL-BINARY-001 rev 2.** The cooperation endings are the stable ones: the three races against Amon in the Void; Jaffa, Tok'ra and Tau'ri against the Goa'uld; Sin and Innocence against Kitava. The side that refuses or forces a union loses: Amon and his hybrids, and the Ori.

## Where it breaks

1. **The assimilator is often a possessed host, not the root adversary** (zerg under Amon). The faction triad conflates the two.
2. **The triad sorts relationships, not species.** The Tok'ra and the Goa'uld are the same species on opposite sides.
3. **Individuals cross roles.** Kerrigan goes from terran to zerg to xel'naga. Vegeta goes from Frieza's soldier (complicit enslavement, CLM-LU-VEG-09, which matches the Jaffa) to Majin to fusion partner.
4. **Consent is not sufficient.** Majin Vegeta consented to Babidi's control, which is consented domination. Symbiosis also needs bounded, shared, revocable control.
5. **Preservation is not automatically safe.** The Khala, the protoss shared link, was the possession surface. The Asgard ended themselves through their own preservation method.
6. **The third faction's origin varies.** It can be a hybrid of both poles (Diablo's Nephalem), an outsider (StarCraft's terrans) or an heir (Stargate's Tau'ri, "the Fifth Race").
7. **The assimilator often comes from the origin layer.** Amon is a xel'naga; the Ori are Alterans; in tradition, Satan is a fallen angel. The assimilator is outside the binary but not outside the origin.
8. **Scripture is not a triad of equals.** Humanity is contested ground in Rev 12, not a third army. A fully independent third faction is a feature of the fiction.
9. **The Replicators have no possessor,** so the possession and consent framing does not apply to them.
10. **Genre convention.** Recurrence across works by different authors may come from a shared genre ancestry rather than independent discovery. This is recorded as untested.

## The open question (t586u)

> Can transformation preserve what is worth preserving without becoming assimilation itself?

In the canon checked, **not by default**. The slide happens each time the third faction treats another party as an instrument. It stays Transformation through consented, bounded alliance. In `SC-GOV-001` terms, measurement rather than indulgence is the guard. This is a COMPILER reading and is untested.

## Default (PROVISIONAL_DEFAULT, pending Shax)

The triad is **RUL-BINARY-001 plus an outside owner**, not a new invariant. Preservation ≈ Oneness and Transformation ≈ Sovereignty.
