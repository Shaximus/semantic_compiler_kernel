## Lore corrections

Secondary transcripts of in-game text were used (poewiki, poedb, Exiled Vault, oriath.net). They are labelled that way in each entry's `outputs.correction`.

1. **Sin and Innocence are twins, not one split god.** Chamber of Innocence stained glass: "On that day two were born of their mother's womb." Act 10, Sin: "You and I are of the same flesh, cleaved from the same womb." The official story is two brothers. The "once a single being who split" frame is interpretation (and is the Piccolo/Kami analogy), not canon.

2. **They do not merge in Act 10.** After Kitava, Sin forgives Innocence; Innocence helps in the fight and then leaves for the south. They remain two persons. This is closer to RUL-BINARY-001's *cooperation* branch than to Namekian fusion.

3. **'He was simply the one who believed the lie the hardest'** was not found in the consulted in-game dialogue (stained glass, Sin/Innocence Act 10, Sin's Act 5–6 lines). Recorded as Hannah/Shax interpretation of the official purity myth versus Innocence's later admission.

4. **Kitava is hunger, not failed compassion.** Kitava's Hunger I (Utula): he offers to watch the feast and eats it; Tukohama blinds him. Sin: "Kitava remembers nothing and cares for nothing but his own hunger and greed." Vilenta: a primal deity of hunger. The feast "kindness" was a lie. The source's "genuinely benevolent god of abundance and care" origin is a lore error.

5. **Kitava resistance penalty is −60%, not a single −30% elemental hit.** After Act 5: −30% to elemental and chaos resistances. After Act 10: another −30% (total −60%). The "lowers future resistance" moral is an interpretation of a progression mechanic.

6. **The Beast's design is Sin's, in his own words.** He planted its seed under Highgate, succumbed himself, and gave it no ambition, which "made it vulnerable to the ambitions of others. Queen Atziri and Doryani. Emperor Chitus and Malachai." Killing it in Act 4 (to stop Malachai) released the gods.

7. **Marauder / Kitava-belly lines are class voicing.** The Marauder has lines such as "Kitava's belly rumbles" on entering the Beast and "Do you hear me, Kitava?!" after Malachai. Every class kills the Beast in Act 4 to stop Malachai. The misidentification is not the quest's causal reason.

8. **Zana's corruption speech is canon.** The source excerpt is slightly abbreviated; the key question is exact: "Is it the corruption that is unique to Wraeclast, or is it the apparent lack of corruption elsewhere that's truly the rarity?"

9. **Vaal chronology.** The Beast already existed when Atziri and Doryani used it (Sin names them; Doryani left an "ancient wound" in its flank). It was not built afterwards as the waste-processing plant the Vaal lacked, and Sin does not describe it as processing thaumaturgic waste.

10. **'Shadow Dragon'** is not a documented Vaal creature in the consulted lore. It is kept only as this source's name for residual accumulation.

11. **Malachai used the Beast; he did not create it.** The Act 4 boss is Malachai, The Nightmare. Tal Rasha is Diablo, not Path of Exile. `SC-MAFUBA-001` is an unvalidated parallel, not a passed test.

## The merger-versus-inversion question (for Shax)

The two entries do **not** have to contradict if they are kept as layers:

| Layer | Entry | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| Failure mode | `SC-POE-DU-001` | Unchecked worship crowns one pole and it becomes a tyrant; the exiled pole can still self-correct. This is a narrative instance of `SC-DU-001` and of a *refused* binary. |
| Stable branch | `SC-POE-MRG-001` | Act 10 cooperation and forgiveness. This is the mutual-cooperation branch of RUL-BINARY-001 rev 2. |

They *do* conflict if both are read as canon origin stories ("one being that split" versus "two brothers"). That conflict is **not** resolved here by fiat. The compiled text withholds split-origin and literal fusion from the canon layer, keeps both entries, and asks:

- Keep `SC-POE-MRG-001` titled "merger" as a metaphor for cooperation?
- Retitle it to cooperation / reconciliation?
- Or demote that layer to a source draft?

Until you rule, both remain compiled SILVER with the layer labels above.
