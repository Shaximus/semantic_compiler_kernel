## 1. SC-LILITH-001 — historical layers

The sequence is not: first wife, then rebellion, then demon. The demonic figure already existed in earlier traditions.

| Layer | What is supported | What is not |
|---|---|---|
| Mesopotamian lilītu / ardat-lilī | Harmful spirits; danger to mothers and infants; sexual aggression; related male and female names. Some scholarship treats the Akkadian names as etymologically distinct from Hebrew laylah ("night"). | A first wife of Adam; a consort of Satan. |
| Isaiah 34:14 | The only Hebrew Bible occurrence of לִילִית (lilit). Not in Genesis. KJV: "screech owl." Other versions: "night hag," "night-monster," or "lilith." | A first-wife story. |
| Late antique incantation bowls | Jewish Babylonian Aramaic bowls, especially Nippur, ca. 5th-8th centuries CE (often 6th-7th). Lilith, male lili-s and female liliths, sometimes expelled by a magical divorce-writ. Predates the first-wife narrative. | The Ben Sira story; a Samael pairing. |
| Alphabet of Ben Sira (generally 8th-10th c.) | First clearly documented first-wife story, as an exegesis of Genesis 1:27 versus Genesis 2. She claims equality, flees, and is bound by an oath about amulets. | A biblical identification. |
| Isaac ha-Kohen, Treatise on the Left Emanation (mid-13th c., a generation before the Zohar) | Lilith paired with Samael as a dark counterpart to the holy pairing. Also: Lilith the Elder (Samael) and Lilith the Younger (Asmodeus). | A uniform identity of Samael with Satan; Lilith as an incarnation of Satan. |
| The Zohar | Develops the left-side pairing. Naamah, in related lists, is a separate figure and is not the first Eve. | A single stable biography. |

Samael is not uniformly Satan. Lilith as consort of an adversarial power has 13th-century historical roots. Lilith as literally another incarnation of Satan is not an established doctrine. Sexual-predatory traits appear in earlier layers; the systematic "succubus of Satan" framing is later. This entry does not place her in a Christ/Satan duality.

## 2. SC-FEM-001 — feminine archetypal lineage

Three hypotheses are kept separate. Only the archetypal one is scored.

| Hypothesis | Status |
|---|---|
| Ontological: one primordial spiritual identity, including the same primordial soul as Christ | Shax's interpretation. Not canon. Not scored as established. |
| Archetypal: multiple figures carrying attributes of one pattern | Scored under SC-AEP-001 as a **CANDIDATE**. |
| Relational: masculine and feminine as a complementary duality | Recorded and not collapsed into the other two. |

Sourced typologies, not visual similarities: Proverbs 8 and 1 Corinthians 1:24, with a majority patristic Wisdom-Christ reading (e.g. Origen, On First Principles 1.2); residual: Irenaeus identifies the Spirit, not the Son, as Wisdom. Mary as New Eve: Justin Martyr, Dialogue with Trypho 100; Irenaeus. Typology, not identity.

Lilith's sovereignty/refusal is a later midrashic attribute, not an early typological partner of Christ in the way Wisdom and the New Eve are. Difference recorded.

### Catherine

Shax did not pick the name. He said the implied figure would be near the beginning of the creation of humans, and that the main point was that Lilith came before Eve.

No tradition was found that places Catherine of Alexandria, Catherine of Siena, or any Catherine near the creation of humans. Catherine of Alexandria is a historically doubtful hagiographic martyr (legend set in the 4th century; later written attestations; removed from the general Roman calendar in 1969). She is a witness figure, not a creation figure.

Not silently substituted: the Gen 1:27 / first-wife slot is already Lilith; Sophia in Proverbs 8 is present at cosmic creation (already the wisdom slot); Naamah (Genesis 4:22; later a separate kabbalistic figure) is not the first Eve and is not a Catherine.

**Default:** keep Catherine of Alexandria as the witness slot, flagged for review, and do not invent a creation-era Catherine.

## 3. SC-SHARD-001 — Usurped-Shard / Captured-Collective

The core pathology is ownership confusion: mistaking access, influence or control for ownership. The decisive transition is when one component claims the right to define the existence, identity or choices of all the others. CANDIDATE, not a class.

Primal Zerg on Zerus (Zurvan, Dehaka) are the unenslaved baseline. Two-stage capture, with a jailbreak residual.

Restoration test: agency regained, coordination without ownership, dissent tolerated, consent, and feedback retained. Linked to `SC-GUARD-001` and `SC-CONSENT-001` (voluntary, informed, agency preserved, revocable).

Shax's Satan/vessel/throne model is interpretation. The Lilith sources do not already demonstrate it.

**Default:** fragmentation plus a feedback break enables the throne claim. External injection (Amon) is a third path. The converse remains open.
