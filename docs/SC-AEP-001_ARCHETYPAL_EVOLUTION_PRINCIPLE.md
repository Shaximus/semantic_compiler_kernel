## 2. The principle

1. **An archetype is a bundle of structural attributes, not a figure.** A character is never "the" Jesus, Satan or Lucifer. It carries some attributes of an older bundle.
2. **Attributes persist across eras and are redistributed, recombined, inverted or expanded.** Each era's vocabulary changes the mechanism; the relationship is what stays recognisable.

   | Older vocabulary | Later vocabulary | Example in this repo |
   |---|---|---|
   | Divine bloodline | Royal genealogy | Jon's R+L legitimacy (`SC-GOT-JON-001`) |
   | Vessel | Genetic compatibility | Supernatural's bloodline vessels (`SC-VESSEL-SPN-001`) |
   | Possession | Parasitic symbiosis | Goa'uld and Tok'ra (`SC-TRIAD-SG-001`) |
   | Resurrection | Biological reconstruction | Qyburn's Gregor (`SC-GOT-MTN-001`) |
   | Fallen angel | Rebel against an authoritarian order | Lucifer pole readings (`SC-LU-VEGETA-001`) |

3. **Composite ("Nephilim") characters** carry several inheritances and gain emergent functions that no single predecessor had. A later story may split one bundle across three characters, fuse five attributes into one, or invert one.
4. **Track three things separately:** *inheritance* (which older structures does the character carry?), *recombination* (which of them combine into something new?), *outcome* (what happens when they interact?).
5. **Safeguard:** a resemblance is a candidate correspondence, not proof of historical transmission or literal identity. Test functions, relationships, transformations and differences, and record the differences. Shared genre ancestry is always a live alternative explanation and is recorded as untested.

### The example bundle (t593u)

Christ-associated attributes: concealed origin; a role imposed by a larger order; protection of the vulnerable; willingness to sacrifice; death or symbolic destruction; return or transformation; reconciliation of opposing forces; authority that does not depend on a throne. `SC-GOT-JON-001` scores Jon against all eight.

## 3. The Nephilim metaphor: where it fits and where it does not

- **TRADITION:** Gen 6:4 names the Nephilim beside the children of "the sons of God" and "the daughters of men": "mighty men which were of old, men of renown". 1 Enoch 6-7 (tradition, not canon for most churches) reads them as Watcher-human offspring. Structurally: a composite of two lineages, with attributes neither parent had.
- **Break:** Genesis does not present the Nephilim as a good composite. They sit beside the corruption that precedes the Flood (Gen 6:5). Shax's metaphor is about composition and emergence, not about morality.
- `SC-NEPH-001` was named in t567u and is not in this repository. It stays unresolved and is not stubbed. The earlier note in `docs/SC-VSR-001_VOLUNTARY_STATE_RECOMPRESSION.md`, that it "may be compositional", agrees with this principle.

## 4. Default (PROVISIONAL_DEFAULT, pending Shax)

t593u sets the method but no threshold. Under the t582u standing rule the compiler picked one:

- A composite correspondence is recorded as a **CANDIDATE** only when **at least three structural attributes are shared**, **at least one is relational** (what the figure does to or with others, not a name, look or title), and **at least one difference is recorded**.
- Below that, the row is **WEAK** or **NAME_BORROWING** (the StarCraft map's existing flag).
- Alternatives not chosen: (a) no threshold, graded only by gates; (b) a majority of the bundle (for example 5 of 8).
