## Where it holds

1. **Corrupted creation, made without consent** (`SC-GOT-001`). The first Walker was a bound captive. The assimilator comes from the origin layer (`SC-TRIAD-001` break 7).
2. **Wights are possession without consent** (`SC-CONSENT-001`); the controller's fall frees all hosts, as with the Neural Parasite (`SC-SCA-POSS-001`).
3. **The liberator becomes the authority she opposed.** Daenerys follows the Terran slide of `SC-TRIAD-SC-001` (Mengsk's rebels, the psi emitters): she burns a city that has surrendered and claims that others "don't get to choose". The mechanism here is removing choice, not owning the enemy.
4. **Restored person versus preserved instrument.** Jon returns able to refuse; Gregor returns as a silent tool; Beric returns a little less each time; in the books Lady Stoneheart returns as vengeance (`SC-GOT-MTN-001`). The revived Gregor fails all four consent conditions, and Qyburn and Cersei hold the outside-owner position of `SC-TRIAD-001`.
5. **The instrument turns on its maker**: Walkers and the Children; Gregor and Qyburn (8x05); the Replicators and Reese (`SC-TRIAD-SG-001`). A candidate recurring structure; genre ancestry is the untested alternative.
6. **David/Goliath inverted.** Oberyn has the range weapon and wins, then puts acknowledgement before the finish (1 Sam 17:46-51 is the reverse order).
7. **Legitimacy from blood, authority from choices** (`SC-GOT-JON-001`).
8. **Burden and crown split.** The Three-Eyed Raven hands the memory to Bran and dies; the Asgard hand their knowledge to the Tau'ri, "the Fifth Race", and end (`SC-TRIAD-SG-001`). The one who carried the war is not the heir.
9. **Cooperation is the stable ending** (RUL-BINARY-001 rev 2). The living unite and survive; the dead and then Daenerys, who accept no shared outcome, are destroyed; the throne is melted and replaced by election.

## Where it breaks

1. **The Night King is not an owner.** He wants an empty world, not subjects. The triad fits on the consent axis only.
2. **The dragons are not a faction.** The show gives them no goal of their own; calling them the third force over-reads them (hence the default).
3. **Jon is not a one-to-one Christ.** He forgives no one, brings back no message, and his last sacrifice is of another. Five of eight bundle attributes, two partial, one inverted (`SC-GOT-JON-001`).
4. **The good side breaks consent too.** Bran, the king, used Hodor's body and broke young Wylis's mind (6x05). The books call taking a human body an abomination.
5. **Gregor was a willing instrument while alive**, and in 8x05 a drive of his own remains.
6. **Jon's resurrection was done to him.** It passes the consent test only because it restores his agency afterwards.
7. **The ecosystem as mapped is the show's.** The Night King, the Walkers' origin, Jon's return, Bran's crown and the burning of King's Landing are not in the published books.
