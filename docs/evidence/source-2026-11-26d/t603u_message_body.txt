### 3. The really interesting matches
Iron Man: the Trapsin. This one is particularly strong. The Assassin's traps are deployed systems that continue operating after placement. Iron Man's suit can deploy drones, automated weapons and defensive countermeasures.

Scarlet Witch: Sorceress plus Necromancer. Sorceress explains the overwhelming magical output. Doctor Strange: Sorceress with Paladin defenses. Teleport is an obvious Sorceress parallel. So are ranged magical attacks and elemental manipulation. Groot: Summoning Druid. Groot is one of the cleanest Druid matches:

* Regeneration and growth.
* Organic manipulation of the environment.

Vision: the constructed vessel. Vision isn't literally a Necromancer, but his artificial body creates a structural parallel to the Necromancer's golems and constructed servants. Now this gets REALLY interesting, because Path of Exile has a different archetypal architecture from Diablo II.
Diablo II gives us seven distinct character classes. Path of Exile takes a more modular approach: seven starting classes, three core attributes, and specialized Ascendancies that turn a shared foundation into very different builds.
So instead of simply asking, "Which Avenger is the Marauder?", we're asking:
What is each Avenger's underlying attribute architecture, and what Ascendancy does their build evolve into?
I'll use original Path of Exile's class and Ascendancy system, with Marvel's MCU Avengers as the character pool. This is a structural interpretation, not an official classification.

### 1. First, the Path of Exile distinction
The seven PoE 1 starting classes are:
| Starting class | Attribute foundation | Three Ascendancies |
| Marauder | Strength | Juggernaut, Berserker, Chieftain |
| Duelist | Strength + Dexterity | Slayer, Gladiator, Champion |
| Ranger | Dexterity | Deadeye, Warden, Pathfinder |
| Shadow | Dexterity + Intelligence | Assassin, Trickster, Saboteur |
| Witch | Intelligence | Occultist, Elementalist, Necromancer |
| Templar | Strength + Intelligence | Inquisitor, Hierophant, Guardian |
| Scion | Strength + Dexterity + Intelligence | Ascendant, Reliquarian, Luminary |
The important mechanical detail: your starting class doesn't lock your skill gems. It determines your starting location on the shared passive tree and which Ascendancies you can specialize into. That's why a Templar can use a bow, or a Witch can build around melee skills. PoE Source +1
That gives us a richer mapping than Diablo II. We can separate the Avenger's underlying attributes from their specialized combat identity.
