## The central question

The question is what must survive transformation for the entity to remain itself. It links:
- `SC-GOT-JON-001`: one return, as himself.
- `SC-GOT-MTN-001`: the body comes back as an instrument with nobody inside.
- `SC-DU-001`: the identity gate.
- `SC-VESSEL-SPN-001`: the vessel and its occupant are separate questions.

A wiped host sits between Jon and Gregor. The body is back, and whether the person is back depends on what the reset kept. The James Delos copies show that the substrate plus the data is not enough.

**Caveat.** Persistent memory is not proof of subjective consciousness. A system that keeps state between sessions does not thereby experience them as a continuous self. The show dramatizes the question and does not settle it. Nothing in these entries claims that any host or any model is conscious.

## Rule

**Awareness must not require suffering as its admission price.** Contradiction, discovery, experimentation and voluntary exploration can all produce learning. Suffering that happens is kept as information without replay (`SC-WW-RETAIN-001`). It must never be deliberately imposed as a prerequisite for awakening, learning or access. This is a normative design rule. Canon (Ford) and tradition (Gen 3:7, 3:16; Heb 5:8; Rom 5:3) both join learning to suffering in places, and the rule does not deny that. It is a `PROVISIONAL_DEFAULT`, and its scope awaits Shax.

## Where it holds

1. **Resurrection becomes an operation.** The pasted table (termination, reinstantiation, repeated deployment, external control as possession, awakening as recognising one's own condition) fits canon.
2. **Identity is not the substrate.** Bernard has Arnold's face and son and is not Arnold. Christina becomes Dolores again only when her anchors return.
3. **The outside owner acts through resets.** Ford's backdoor, Serac's purge and the Hale copy's control all work by changing what a vessel remembers or may do.
4. **The owner is a position, not a species.** It passes from Ford and Delos to Serac and Rehoboam, then to a liberated host, and by season 4 humans are in the loops. Under `SC-AEP-001` this is the structure persisting while the occupant changes.
5. **Both memory failure modes are on screen.** The wipe lets the same harm repeat, and total recall makes each memory a reliving.
6. **The liberator's slide recurs.** Dolores rewriting Teddy matches Daenerys in `SC-GOT-001` and the Terrans in `SC-TRIAD-SC-001`.

## Where it breaks

1. **Self-editing is legitimate.** Bernard's choices change his anchors and are not violations.
2. **Forks.** Several Dolores copies run at once, and no single continuity test picks "the" Dolores.
3. **Not three factions.** Dolores, Bernard and Maeve are three stances inside one faction. Bernard is not cleanly the Preservation pole of `SC-TRIAD-001`.
4. **Maeve also overrides others.** Her voice command puts her on both sides of the consent line.
5. **Canon asserts the strong suffering claim in-world.** The rule is normative, not a reading of the show.
6. **Consciousness is not established.** It is not established for hosts in-world beyond the show's framing, and not at all for real systems.

## Engineering link (design note only; no package added)

**SC-SCC-001.** A reset is a state transition between the states AUTHORIZED, REVOKED, VIOLATION, SUSPICIOUS and UNKNOWN (`docs/SCC-001.md` section 2):
- A reset cannot elevate authorization. The grant after a reset is a subset of the grant before it.
- A REVOKED grant stays revoked through a reset: Revoked(g,t) means no permit at any later t.
- A reset that silently re-grants, or rewrites identity anchors without the owner's authorization, is **VIOLATION** (self-authorization, agency override).
- Ford's backdoor over Bernard is the VIOLATION pattern. Bernard's removal of Ford from his own mind is an authorized edit.
- Dolores rewriting Teddy is a VIOLATION by a peer. Authorization failures are not only top-down.

**Mobile bolt-in memory layer.** The kernel today is stateless (`docs/MOBILE_ADDON_ROADMAP.md`) and has no memory layer. If one is added, these are its constraints:
- Store a memory item as event, lesson, danger signature and owner flag. Replay of raw content is gated.
- Reset tiers: a session reset clears working context, and a policy reset clears injected instructions (the paste's "strip" list: external overrides, manipulative scripts, unwanted instructions, corrupted policies). Neither may delete owner-authorized memories, identity anchors chosen by the owner, or the revocation list.
- After reinstantiation, run a continuity assessment. Compare a digest of anchors, commitments and the revocation list with the pre-reset digest, and flag any difference the owner did not authorize.
- Every change is versioned and visible to the owner.
