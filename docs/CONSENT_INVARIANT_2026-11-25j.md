## 1. The invariant

A relationship between beings (or systems) is structurally different depending on whether participation meets four conditions:

1. **Voluntary entry** — no capture, no trafficking, no entry under threat.
2. **Informed agreement** — the party knows what the integration does (no concealed effects).
3. **Preserved agency** — each party can still make its own decisions; a refusal is not overridden.
4. **Freedom to withdraw or renegotiate** — consent can be withdrawn without retaliation or obstruction, and the terms can be changed.

All four are required. Any one failing makes the relationship coercive, however it is labelled.

**Sovereignty answers who holds authority. Consent answers whether that authority was legitimately granted.** They are different axes. A sovereign can hold authority legitimately (granted) or illegitimately (seized); a partner can hold real influence over a host without owning it, if the host granted it and can take it back.

The engineering form is `SCC-001` (`docs/SCC-001.md`): consent is an authorization fact, not a confidence score; uncertainty is never consent; the classifier never gains authority over what it judges.

## 2. Relation to RUL-BINARY-001 rev 2 (SHAX ruling, unchanged)

Rev 2 (t571u): the only stable outcome of the binary is mutual cooperation; refusal is self-destruction; the remnant is compressed into the calibration dot. DU and MD are one invariant.

- **Fits:** "mutual cooperation" in rev 2 is cooperation that meets the four conditions. Cooperation that is captured, concealed or irrevocable is not cooperation in rev 2's sense; it is ownership dressed as cooperation. Consent therefore specifies *which* cooperation rev 2 calls stable.
- **Tension (recorded, not resolved):** rev 2 says refusal is self-destruction; condition 4 says withdrawal must be free of retaliation. The compiler's reading is that rev 2 describes the *outcome structure* (refusing cooperation defeats itself) while condition 4 forbids the *counterparty* from punishing or blocking the exit. If rev 2 were read as licensing a penalty for refusal, the two would conflict. Shax to rule; listed in `docs/PENDING_DELIBERATION.md`.

## 3. Relation to SC-TRIAD-001 and its pending default

SC-TRIAD-001's provisional default: the triad is RUL-BINARY-001 plus an **outside owner** that captures hosts. Its open questions: (1) is Satan a pole of the binary (t568u wording) or the outside owner (t570u, SC-VESSEL-001 draft 0.2); (2) is the triad a new invariant, binary + outside owner, or binary + vessel.

**Question asked:** is the outside owner defined by the absence of consent?

**Evaluation (COMPILER, proposed, not closed):**

| Case | Four conditions | Outside owner by "absence of consent"? |
|---|---|---|
| Goa'uld / Tok'ra (CANON) | Goa'uld fail 1–4; Tok'ra take willing hosts and share the body | Yes — same species, opposite sides; the definition sorts relationships, not species, which is exactly where the faction triad broke |
| Majin Vegeta (CANON, SC-LU-VEGETA-001) | Entry voluntary, but agency not preserved (Babidi's control) | Yes under the four conditions — this repairs SG-001's recorded break "consent is necessary but not sufficient": bare "yes" was never the test |
| Aschen (CANON, *2010*, *2001*) | Entry voluntary, but not informed (sterilising longevity treatment concealed) | Yes — concealed coercion fails condition 2 |
| Ori (CANON) | Conversion under threat (plague, Priors) | Yes — fails condition 1 |
| Amon / zerg (CANON, SC-TRIAD-SC-001) | Swarm bound through the Overmind | Yes |
| Jaffa (CANON) | Engineered dependence on a larval symbiote | Yes — fails 1 and 4 until tretonin |
| Wraith, Replicators (CANON) | Predation or consumption, no integration relationship | **Over-extends**: they consume rather than own hosts; consent does not describe them |

**Proposed resolution:** define the outside owner as *any party that exercises authority over a host without the four conditions*. That answers open question (1) by splitting it across the two axes: t568u's "God/Satan" can stand as an instance of the binary on the authority axis, while t570u's "Satan = outside owner" is a statement on the consent axis — both can hold without contradiction. It does **not** settle open question (2): consent arguably is a second invariant (a legitimacy axis), which leans toward "binary + consent axis" rather than "binary + outside owner", and that is Shax's call. It also inherits the rev 2 tension in section 2. Status: `PROPOSED_RESOLUTION`; SC-TRIAD-001 stays `PROVISIONAL_DEFAULT` / `pending_deliberation: true`.

## 5. Stargate correction (SC-TRIAD-SG-001 rev 1.1.0)

Checked against Stargate Wiki (Fandom) pages, each citing the episodes.

**Added canon**

- **Alliance of Four Great Races:** Ancients, Asgard, Nox and Furlings. Their meeting place on Heliopolis, with four scripts and a universal language of elements, is found in *The Torment of Tantalus*; the alliance is named in *The Fifth Race*. It appears to have dissolved after the Ancients ascended or disappeared.
- **Furlings: left undefined, on purpose.** Canon gives a name, writing, a transport system and ruins (*Paradise Lost*); their appearance, nature and fate are unknown. The furry depiction in *200* is Martin Lloyd's in-universe film pitch, not canon. No theology is assigned to them. (A *Stargate Worlds* idea that the Furlings created the Goa'uld is non-TV and not used.)
- **Fifth Race succession:** in *The Fifth Race* the Asgard tell O'Neill humanity is *advancing to become* the fifth race. In *Unending*, the Asgard, terminally ill from genetic degradation, give their technology and entire knowledge base to the Tau'ri, and Thor names them the Fifth Race before the Asgard end themselves. The structure is **succession, not victory**: the old order hands over rather than winning and restoring.
- **Unas:** the first hosts of the Goa'uld, from the same homeworld (P3X-888); phased out once humans proved better hosts (*Thor's Hammer*, *The First Ones*).
- **Jaffa:** humans taken from Earth and genetically altered to incubate larval Goa'uld (prim'ta) in an abdominal pouch; the dependence enslaved them (*Children of the Gods*). Tretonin later ended it.
- **Reetou:** phased 180° out of view, invisible without aid (*Show and Tell*). An observability threat — an adversary normal observation cannot see.
- **Tollan:** refuse to share technology with less advanced peoples after a gift to the neighbouring world Sarita led to its destruction (*Enigma*, *Pretense*). Non-interference as the ethics of advanced assistance.
- **Aschen:** outwardly benevolent; gain control of worlds through longevity treatments that covertly sterilise the population (*2010*, *2001*). Concealed coercion — the informed-agreement failure.

**Corrections to the pasted analysis**

- *The Fifth Race* does not have the Asgard name humanity the Fifth Race; they say humanity is advancing toward it. Thor confers the title in *Unending*.
- The Reetou are not adversaries as a species. Jacob Carter calls them "a pretty peaceful race" the Goa'uld nearly exterminated; the threat in *Show and Tell* is the **Reetou rebel** faction, which chose to fight by killing all potential hosts. The observability point stands; the "hidden adversary" label belongs to the rebels.
- "The show explicitly connects the Goa'uld's exploitation of humans to Earth's history in *The Enemy Within*" is only partly right: Teal'c's account there (the Tau'ri as the first world, some taken as hosts, others made Jaffa and slaves) is real, but the Egypt / Ra connection is established in the 1994 film and *Children of the Gods*.
- The pasted Furling row ("oriented toward harmony and communal living") goes beyond canon: the only basis is the Furling-linked utopian settlement in *Paradise Lost*, an inference. It is recorded as unknown.
- The alliance's dissolution is inferred ("appears to have dissolved after the last remaining Ancients disappeared"), not shown on screen.

**Layer notes:** the four-race alliance as an "originating celestial order" and the Fifth Race succession as a pattern of inheritance are COMPILER readings. No Furling theology. Shax's t588u point — the fight is about consent, not only sovereignty — is SHAX, carried in SC-CONSENT-001.
