## How to register

1. Open a GitHub issue with the [usage-registration template](https://github.com/Shaximus/semantic_compiler_kernel/issues/new?template=usage_registration.md), or
2. Email `shaximusoverride6@gmail.com` with the subject `[USAGE REGISTRATION] semantic_compiler_kernel - <organization or product>`.

Please include, as far as you are willing to share:

- Organization or product name
